Chess • Community • Character

More than a game.

A welcoming place to play and grow.

The King’s Chess Club brings people together over a game that rewards patience, creativity, sportsmanship, and thoughtful decisions. Hosted at 3 Cord Ministries, our club is also a Christian outreach. We hope to share Christ’s love through kindness, hospitality, encouragement, and genuine friendship.

Everyone is welcome, whatever your faith background—or if you have no religious faith. Come enjoy chess, ask questions, or simply get to know us. There’s no pressure to participate in a faith conversation; we want every visitor to feel respected and at home.

  • Casual games in a relaxed atmosphere
  • Beginners can learn at their own pace
  • All ages and playing strengths are welcome
  • Come to play, learn, watch, or meet people
Pull up a chair

There’s room for you here.

You don’t need to be a strong player—or even know all the rules—to get started. Bring your curiosity and we’ll help you find your place at the board. Our hope is to make everyone feel welcome through kindness, encouragement, and genuine friendship.

Love the game?

Find a friendly opponent, try a new strategy, or enjoy a thoughtful game with fellow chess fans.

Just getting started?

Beginners are welcome. Ask questions, learn the pieces, and build confidence one move at a time.

A club for all ages

Chess can bring generations together. Young players are welcome with a parent or caregiver’s support.

Please check with a parent or guardian about arrangements for children attending.
